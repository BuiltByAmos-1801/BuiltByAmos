About Built By Amos · India

Fashion-first digital experiences, built with care.

Built By Amos is an independent digital studio helping ambitious fashion and lifestyle brands build a stronger online presence through distinctive digital storefronts, thoughtful responsive experiences, and clear communication.

The studio

Strategy, design, and delivery under one roof.

From shaping the first idea to preparing for launch, the focus is on creating a digital experience that feels like the brand and works for its customers.

That means pairing fashion-aware visual direction with useful structure, responsive design, and a straightforward project process. The studio can also support app experiences and custom tools where a project calls for them.
